# Sources and adaptations

Original synthesis of the material collected for `khorikov-unit-testing` and `requirements-tdd`, 2026-10-23. This skill includes its necessary guidance and does not require either companion skill or an upstream plugin.

- Vladimir Khorikov: [How to Assert Database State](https://enterprisecraftsmanship.com/posts/how-to-assert-database-state/), [When to Mock](https://enterprisecraftsmanship.com/posts/when-to-mock/), [unmanaged dependencies](https://khorikov.org/posts/2021-11-29-unmanaged-dependencies-explained/), [independent test knowledge](https://enterprisecraftsmanship.com/posts/leaking-domain-knowledge-tests/), [DRY and DAMP](https://enterprisecraftsmanship.com/posts/dry-damp-unit-tests/). Foundation: regression protection, refactor resistance, fast feedback, maintenance cost and client-relative observations. The earlier research used an official chapter excerpt and author articles, not the complete book.
- [Matt Pocock TDD](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/tdd/SKILL.md), inspected installed plugin 1.3.1: behavior-focused seams and independent expectations. We assess a seam from its actual contract rather than rejecting every interaction or database observation.
- [Poteto / Noodle testing](https://github.com/poteto/noodle/blob/82d2921c52370f23f29086de81ccfb600939c037/.agents/skills/testing/SKILL.md): concrete reproduction and focused regression evidence. Project commands, fixtures and automatic commits are excluded.
- [Superpowers TDD](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b/skills/test-driven-development/SKILL.md): verify the relevant reason for failure and the unchanged test's later result. Final test quality is assessed separately from unobserved TDD history.
- [Everything Claude Code TDD](https://github.com/affaan-m/everything-claude-code/blob/90606c8315ee09cafc303515c5f33024db6b9882/skills/tdd-workflow/SKILL.md): connect requirements, executable guarantees and actual commands. Universal coverage quotas and assertion-count rules are not used as quality measures.

The qualitative verdicts, evidence levels, review report and disposable probe workflow are agent-oriented adaptations. Previous experiments informed distinctions between assertion credit, domain exceptions, setup errors, healthy faulty runs and false refactor failures. Those experiments do not establish universal validity, and their private fixture answers are not embedded here. Upstream ideas are expressed in original prose, without copying their skill bodies or source code.

The 2026-10-24 update also draws on [Anthropic / Theorem verification research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification) and the [OSS Verify enrollment skill](https://github.com/theorem-labs/oss-verify/blob/abd10afe7dfc32050a08375c3eb9e2f332025152/.claude/skills/add-project/SKILL.md). The property-evidence questions adapt those ideas to reviews with healthy controls; missing formal tooling is not a test defect.
